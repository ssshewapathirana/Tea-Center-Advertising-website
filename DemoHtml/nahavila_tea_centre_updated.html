What tea gives you

Why drinkers come back.

Beyond the taste — the quiet benefits and the interesting numbers behind every cup of Ceylon tea, in plain language.

01 · Steady focus

Calm alertness, no crash

Tea’s gentle caffeine travels with L-theanine, an amino acid that smooths the lift — clear, settled focus that lasts, without the coffee jitters or the mid-afternoon dip.

02 · Antioxidants

A cup full of flavonoids

Ceylon tea is naturally rich in polyphenol antioxidants — the family of compounds studied for supporting heart health and helping the body cope with everyday stress.

03 · Light & refreshing

Flavour, almost zero calories

Plain tea is virtually calorie-free and mostly water — a naturally hydrating ritual that refreshes without sugar or additives. Straight, with lemon, or with a splash of milk.

04 · All-day ritual

One for every hour

Brisk black at breakfast, soft green after lunch, silver tips for slow evenings — tea’s gentler caffeine curve fits the whole day. A small, repeatable pleasure.
